PhotoSizeView Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

PhotoSizeView ("the app") is designed to keep your photos and personal information private. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to that information.

Summary

PhotoSizeView does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Photo library access

PhotoSizeView asks for permission to access your photo library so it can:

  • display your photos and videos along with their file sizes,
  • sort and filter them, and
  • delete the items you choose.

Your photos, videos, and their details (such as file size, dimensions, and dates) are read only on your device and are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone. Items are deleted only when you choose to delete them and confirm in the system prompt. Deleted items are moved to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app.

You can change or revoke photo access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > PhotoSizeView. You can also grant access to only selected photos.

iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos, iOS may download thumbnails or previews from iCloud when you view them in the app. These downloads are handled by Apple's Photos framework under Apple's own privacy policy. PhotoSizeView does not receive or store your iCloud account information.

Data collection

PhotoSizeView does not:

  • require an account or sign-in,
  • collect analytics or usage data,
  • show advertising,
  • track you across apps or websites, or
  • include any third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's privacy

PhotoSizeView does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact via the app store support.
